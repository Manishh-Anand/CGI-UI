# Roles and onboarding

## Key roles

- Client Delivery Lead: owns the matter budget, timeline and client relationship; signs off every phase gate.
- Review Manager: runs the reviewer team, quality gates and TAR calibration on a day-to-day basis.
- Processing Lead: owns Forge pipeline runs, exception handling and load-file generation.
- TAR Specialist: designs and monitors the TAR workflow, seed sets and model validation.
- Solution Architect: designs the matter's technical landscape; the person who decides platform configuration, hosting and security settings.
- Forensic Examiner: handles imaging, hashing and chain of custody in Forensic & Collection.

## What new joiners need to know

The most common onboarding questions are about how the departments fit together, where a given document or dashboard lives, and how a matter moves from collection to production. The intelligence assistant is being built to answer exactly these questions from the firm's own knowledge base instead of sending new joiners on a department-by-department tour.

## Escalation and ownership

If a deadline is at risk, the escalation path is: review manager to client delivery lead within the same day, then to the delivery director if the risk persists beyond a working day. Processing capacity conflicts escalate through the Data Operations lead. Everything that touches a client deliverable ultimately reports to the client delivery lead.
