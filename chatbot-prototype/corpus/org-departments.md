# Departments and organisation

## Department map

The firm is organised into eight departments. Review Operations runs the review teams and TAR workflows. Data Operations (called "Processing" internally) runs the Forge pipeline and the processing grid. Forensic & Collection handles imaging, chain of custody and mobile collection. Client Delivery is account management and project management. Solution Architecture designs matter-specific technical setups. The Platform & IT team owns Iris, Forge, infrastructure and security. Finance & Billing runs accruals and invoicing. People & Talent handles hiring, training and reviewer onboarding. All staffing figures here are synthetic planning values.

## Sizes and location

Review Operations is the largest department by headcount; it absorbs the reviewer pool that is flexed across matters. Data Operations is smaller but carries the biggest infrastructure risk during peak weeks. Platform & IT is split between the review platform team and the pipeline team. The firm runs from a central office in London, with an offshore review centre on the east coast of the United States and a small forensic lab in Frankfurt. The offshore centre handles the bulk of volume review overnight.

## How departments interact at matter level

Every matter has a dedicated matter team drawn from these departments: a client delivery lead, a review manager, a processing lead, a TAR specialist, and one or more solution architects for technical setup. The matter team reports into the client delivery lead, who owns the budget. Capacity disputes between matters are arbitrated by the delivery directors weekly. This matrix structure is the reason onboarding covers both departmental process and matter-team behaviour.
