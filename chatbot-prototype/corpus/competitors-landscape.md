# Competitor landscape

## Market shape

The eDiscovery services market contains a small number of full-service providers and a long tail of niche shops. The competitive dynamic that matters most is convergence: the platform vendors are pushing into managed services, and the services firms are pushing into software. The names, figures and rankings in this document are fictional stand-ins used for planning; they must not be quoted as market facts.

## Fictional competitors (stand-ins)

- Aurelian Discovery: the largest full-service competitor, strong in banking-sector work, with an aggressive fixed-fee pricing model.
- Verita Processing Co: processing-led, positionally cheap on high-volume processing, weaker in review quality at scale.
- Lattice eDiscovery: mid-size, strong TAR analytics reputation, popular with law-firm clients.
- MeridianGrid: challenger focused on cloud-native collection from chat and collaboration platforms.
- FirstPoint Legal Tech: boutique, premium-priced, specialises in privilege review and white-collar matters.

## Where we win and lose

The firm wins on defensible, auditable process and on deadline discipline in regulated matters. It loses most often on price against Aurelian's fixed-fee bids and on breadth of software features against the platform-first competitors. Win/loss analysis on recent pitches shows the two top reasons clients chose the firm were "delivered before" and "process documentation". The top reason clients chose someone else was price on mid-size matters.

## Watching

Two competitor moves are being tracked this quarter: Aurelian's push into managed services pricing and MeridianGrid's expansion into processing. If Aurelian's fixed-fee model spreads, the firm's own pricing for large matters will need revisiting at the next planning cycle.
