# Matter Meridian — flagship banking investigation

## Engagement

Matter Meridian (fictional stand-in for a real engagement) is an investigation into a banking client's cross-border transaction records, driven by a regulatory inquiry. The client engaged the firm for collection, processing, review and production under an extremely compressed regulatory timeline. All figures in this document are synthetic planning values.

## What was delivered

Collection covered forty-two custodians across three regions, including laptop images, cloud mailboxes and chat archives. Processing normalised just over forty terabytes of data; after near-duplicate and email threading the review population was roughly nine hundred thousand documents. Review used TAR with a senior-second-level seed set. The team produced two tranches: a first regulatory response within the hard deadline and a supplementary production three weeks later. The production included a full privilege log and a load file in the regulator's mandated format.

## Team and staffing

Peak staffing on the matter was around one hundred and forty reviewers across two shifts, with a processing team of twelve and a dedicated TAR specialist. The matter ran close to the top of the agreed budget: management flagged scope creep early when the client added a third region mid-review, and account management renegotiated the budget before the additional data was processed.

## Outcomes

The regulators accepted the first response without follow-up requests, and the client renewed for a related matter immediately afterwards. The win/loss note for Matter Meridian is used in sales conversations as the reference case for delivering under regulatory deadlines. Because it was a flagship, the dashboards carry Matter Meridian as a named example of on-time, on-budget delivery.
